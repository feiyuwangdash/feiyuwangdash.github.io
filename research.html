Negotiated Trade Protection: Minimum Import Prices and Quantity Commitments

Abstract: Negotiated Trade Protection: Minimum Import Prices and Quantity Commitments

I study how negotiated alternatives to tariffs affect firm incentives, consumer demand, and the distribution of surplus. I combine a three-stage proposal, acceptance, and pricing game with a random-coefficients demand model and multiproduct price competition in the European electric vehicle market. In the counterfactuals, quantity commitments yield slightly higher exporter profits than minimum import prices, while minimum import prices deliver higher social welfare and EV sales.

Targeted Tariffs and Product Portfolios: Evidence from the European Electric Vehicle Market

Abstract: Targeted Tariffs and Product Portfolios: Evidence from the European Electric Vehicle Market

Where does tariff incidence appear when a policy covers only part of a firm’s portfolio? I study the EU’s duties on Chinese-built battery-electric vehicles using model-by-country registrations and annual posted prices for 2023–2025. EU markets record 6.2 fewer Chinese-built BEV models per destination-month relative to comparison markets, measured by active registration spells. Holding model reference prices fixed, the registered product mix shifts toward higher-priced vehicles, with a 12.1% increase in the German-reference index. These findings document adjustment in product-market presence and the composition of registrations.

Sequential Visits: Measuring Consumer Visit Patterns

With Sandeep Chitla, Jingwei Zhang, and Ruihao Zhu

Abstract: Sequential Visits: Measuring Consumer Visit Patterns

We study how to measure habitual consumer visits using mobile-location data. Using 1.03 billion visit records spanning 15 months, we compare repeat visits to the same location with visits to different locations. Sharp weekly timing patterns appear in both groups, showing that weekly recurrence alone does not identify habitual return behavior. This comparison guides the construction of samples for studying consumer visit patterns.
